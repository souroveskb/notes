## Kaizen Method 
Motivation is not the spark that creates the action. Action brings momentum. Doing when I am motivated means I have to wait to get the motivation, spark. But doing regularly and consistently will give me the confidence that I need. Even if the progress is slow, it will become solid foundation. Waiting for motivation means handing over my life to emotion.
