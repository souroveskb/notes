 ML models train on conditional probabilities. P(X=x) can change drastically when we get to know Y=y. That's how the ML models work? 
 P(X=x | Y=y) or P(x|y) Consider X = having flu, Y = high temperature
 From my understanding the conditions or Y are mainly features based on which the model comes up with a probability distribution.

Idea of Deep learning models is -> we feed them X-rays and let them provide diagnosis. It sees some evidence and gives us a diagnosis. 

Independence 
Events A and B are independent if P(A,B) = P(A)P(B) or P(A|B) = P(A) or P(B|A) = P(B)
Events A and B are conditionally independent given X if 
P(A|B,C) = P(A|C)
P(B|A,C) = P(B|C)
P(A,B|C) = P(A|C)P(B|C)

If X is continuous then the probability distribution is called the density function f.
$\int_{a}^{b} f(x) \,dx$ and $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(x)dx=1$

Marginalization is the calculation of probability using law of the total probability. It is called marginal probability.
