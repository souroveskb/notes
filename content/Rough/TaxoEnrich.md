## TaxoEnrich: Self-Supervised Taxonomy Completion via Structure-Semantic Representations
This is a taxonomy completion framework, which effectively leverages both semantic features and structural information in the existing taxonomy and offers a better representation of candidate position to boost the performance of taxonomy completion.
