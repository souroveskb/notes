## LLM-GAN: Construct Generative Adversarial Network Through Large Language Models For Explainable Fake News Detection

A framework that utilizes prompting mechanisms to enable LLM to become generator and detector for realistic fake news generation and detection.
![[Fake_news_LLM_GAN.png]]

These are the cases that were shown in the paper as examples of LLM-GAN where the strongest baseline model failed. LLM-GAN made correct predictions and provided logical human readable explanations as evidence. Errors in fake news and key information in explanations are highlighted with a gray background .
